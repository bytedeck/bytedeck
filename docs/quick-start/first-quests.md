# Your first quests

A **quest** is a lesson or assignment: instructions for the student, and a submission they hand in. When you approve the submission, the student earns the quest's XP, and the quests that follow it become available.

## The starter quests

Choose **Quests** in the left menu. A new deck already has a few quests, ready for your students:

![The teacher's Quests page: Create and Bulk Edit buttons, Available, Drafts and Archived tabs, and the starter quests with their XP, campaign and tags](../images/quick-start/quests-list.png)
/// caption
The Quests page on a new deck, listing its starter quests.
///

* **Welcome to ByteDeck!** is every student's first quest. It's approved automatically as soon as they submit it. Edit it to introduce your class: it's the first thing students read.
* Submitting it opens the **Orientation** campaign: **ByteDeck Class Contract**, **Create an Avatar**, **Screenshots** and **Who owns your creations?**. These teach students how to submit work, and you approve each one.
* Completing all four earns the **ByteDeck Proficiency** badge, which opens **Send your teacher a Message**, a quest students can repeat whenever they want to reach you.

Use these as they are, edit them, or archive the ones you don't want. To open a quest, click its row, then use the buttons that appear to view, edit, copy or archive it.

## Create a quest

On the Quests page, press **Create**. The form is long, but a first quest needs only seven of its fields:

![The top of the Create New Quest form, with Name, Xp, Published and Verification required marked 1 to 4](../images/quick-start/quest-create.png)
/// caption
The top of the Create New Quest form.
///

![Further down the form: the Quest Details, Submission Instructions and Instructor notes editors, marked 5 to 7](../images/quick-start/quest-create-details.png)
/// caption
Further down the same form.
///

1. **Name**: what students see in their list of quests.
2. **XP**: how much XP the student earns when the quest is approved.
3. **Published**: leave it ticked for students to see the quest. Untick it to keep working on it: it waits in your **Drafts** tab until you publish it.
4. **Verification required**: leave it ticked to review each submission before the student gets the XP. Untick it for quests that should be approved automatically, like the Welcome quest.
5. **Quest Details**: everything the student needs to do the quest: instructions, images, videos and links.
6. **Submission Instructions**: what the student should hand in, and how.
7. **Instructor notes**: notes only teachers can see, such as an answer key or what to look for.

Press **Create**, at the top of the form, to save the quest.

!!! tip "Ask for specific answers"
    To give each thing you want back its own answer field (a short answer, a long answer or a file), add [submission questions](../quests/submission-questions.md) to the quest once it's saved.

!!! warning "New quests wait for the ByteDeck Proficiency badge"
    Under **Basic Prerequisites** at the bottom of the form, every new quest starts with the **ByteDeck Proficiency** badge as its prerequisite, so students see it only after they finish the Orientation campaign. To make a quest available as soon as a student joins, clear that badge before you press Create. To change the starting prerequisite for all new quests, use **Default quest prerequisite** in Site Configuration.

## What students see

A student's **Available** tab lists the quests open to them:

![A student's Available tab with Welcome to ByteDeck! opened (marked 1), showing its short description and the View Details and Start Quest (marked 2) buttons](../images/quick-start/student-start-quest.png)
/// caption
A new student's Available tab, with their first quest.
///

1. They click a quest to read its summary.
2. They press **Start Quest**.

The quest moves to their **In Progress** tab. They read it, then hand in their work with the submission form at the bottom:

![The Quest Submission Form: the editor with the student's answer (1), Choose files (2), Save Draft (3) and Submit Quest for Approval (4)](../images/quick-start/student-submit.png)
/// caption
The submission form at the bottom of a quest the student has started.
///

1. They write their answer in the editor.
2. They attach any files with **Choose files**.
3. **Save Draft** keeps their work without submitting it, so they can come back later.
4. **Submit Quest for Approval** hands it in. A quest that needs your approval now waits for you.
