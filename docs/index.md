# ByteDeck User Guide

ByteDeck is a gamified learning management system: your students work through **quests** (lessons and assignments), earn **XP** and **badges** as you approve their work, and climb **ranks** as their XP grows. Each class, school or program runs its own **deck**, a private ByteDeck site at its own address such as `yourdeck.bytedeck.com`.

This guide is for the people who run a deck: the deck owner and the teachers who help run it.

## Words you'll see

Deck
:   Your own ByteDeck site, with its own students, content and settings.

Deck owner
:   The person who requested the deck. The owner can change every setting and add other teachers.

Teacher
:   A staff member on the deck: teachers create content and review student work. Students are everyone else.

Quest
:   A lesson or assignment. A student starts a quest, submits their work, and earns the quest's XP once it is approved.

Campaign
:   A group of related quests, like a unit in a course.

Badge
:   An achievement a student earns, either automatically by meeting its prerequisites or from a teacher.

Prerequisite
:   What a student must do before a quest or badge becomes available to them, such as completing another quest or earning a badge.

XP
:   Experience points. Students earn XP for approved quests and some badges, and their XP sets their rank.
