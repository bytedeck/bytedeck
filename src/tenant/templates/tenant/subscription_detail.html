How subscriptions work

{# the full lifecycle, so an owner always knows what happens next (maintainer request, 2026-07-30) #}
  1. Valid subscription: full use of the deck at your subscription level.
  2. Grace period: when a trial or subscription expires, everything keeps working as before for {{ grace_days }} more days while you subscribe or renew.
  3. Suspension: after the grace period, the deck is suspended. Only the deck owner can sign in, and the 365-day countdown to deck deletion begins. Content and student data stay intact.
  4. Deletion: a deck with no valid subscription for 365 days may be permanently deleted.

Not using the deck right now? The low-cost Maintenance subscription keeps it online indefinitely with trial-level limits (max {{ trial_cap }} current student{{ trial_cap|pluralize }}) and stops the deletion countdown (ideal for building or testing between active use).
