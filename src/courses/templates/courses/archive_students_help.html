Your deck's subscription is based on the number of current students. Students on a deck are in one of three states:

  • Current — registered in a course in the active semester. Only current students count toward your deck's limit. Staff never count.
  • Active — every other student: they can still sign in and browse, but they aren't registered in a course this semester, so they don't count.
  • Inactive (archived) — students marked as not active on their profile. They can't sign in, and they appear under their own Inactive tab on the Students page.

End the active semester (whole class at once)

When a semester ends, close it from the Semesters page using End Active Semester. All of its course registrations are deactivated, so those students go from current to merely active — they stop counting toward your limit as soon as a new semester becomes active, while still being able to sign in. This is the normal end-of-term workflow.

Archive individual students

To archive a single student, edit their profile and un-check Active. Archived (inactive) students can't sign in and never count toward your limit; their work and XP are kept, and re-activating them restores everything. Find them under the Inactive tab of the Students list.

When do the numbers update?

The counts shown on banners refresh nightly. Enforcement when a student registers in a course always uses a live count, so freeing a seat (either way above) takes effect right away even if a banner still shows the old number.
